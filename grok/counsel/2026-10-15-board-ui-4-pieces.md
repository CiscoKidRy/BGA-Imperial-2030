## Shapes actually seen

On `1129209.jpg`, `904577.jpg`, and `1009471.jpg` the pieces fall into five shapes.

Factory blocks are plain wooden blocks. Some are brown. Some are light blue. They sit on city marks. A fleet is a small ship silhouette, green and blue in these photos, and it sits on the sea. A flag is a small square tile with a printed flag, on land and on sea. A rondel marker is a short cylinder standing on a wedge. Colors seen on the wheels include red, blue, green, yellow, purple, and black. An orange wooden pawn stands on the tax chart, not on the map and not on the rondel. On `1129209.jpg` it is on the +5 cell above Brazil; the head and base read as two orange rounds from that angle. The same orange pawn is on that chart in `904577.jpg` and `1009471.jpg`.

`1009471.jpg` has spare piles beside the board. The green pile and the blue pile are ship-shaped, with flag tiles mixed in. The red pile and the purple pile are rectangular blocks, also with flag tiles. One black block sits with the green ships. On the map, nation-colored land pieces are the same kind of small block.

Armies and factories cannot be told apart by outline in these photos. Brown and light blue mark the factory blocks. Nation color marks the other blocks. Only the fleets have a different silhouette.

`575912.jpg` draws brown blocks, light-blue blocks, ship models, small flags, and cylinders on the rondel. No orange pawn is in that frame. It is not a wooden-piece photo.

## City marks on the empty board

On `574713.jpg` a city mark is either brown or light blue, and each color is either a square plaque or a round badge. The name sits on a pale label beside the mark. These are the marks that are readable. No shape is given for any other name.

Brown square: Chicago, Paris, Moscow, Beijing, New Delhi, Brasilia.

Brown circle: Berlin, Novosibirsk, Urumqi, Chongqing, Chennai, Manaus.

Light-blue square: New Orleans, London, Vladivostok, Shanghai, Mumbai, Rio de Janeiro.

Light-blue circle: San Francisco, New York, Rome, Murmansk, Kolkata, Fortaleza.

The red cross near Rome is printed on the board. It is not a city mark.

## Drawing recipe

Use simple original shapes. Do not redraw the picture inside a city plaque, and do not redraw a printed flag.

- Armament factory: a brown square.
- Shipyard: a light-blue square. The empty board's light-blue cities are not all squares. The piece on the test screen is still a square, so it is not read as sea.
- Fleet: a small ship silhouette in the nation color.
- Flag: a pin with the nation letter, not a copied flag. Letters: Russia R, China C, India I, Brazil B, United States U, Europe E.
- Rondel: a cylinder standing on the wedge, in the nation color.

Nation colors: Russia purple, China yellow, India black, Brazil green, United States red, Europe blue.

City names stay on light labels so they remain readable on yellow, red, and black land.

A region with no piece still shows its name. Only the engine's legal regions get a click outline.
