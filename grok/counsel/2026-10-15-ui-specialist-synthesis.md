# How the six reviews were used, 2026-10-15

The six reviews are the files beside this note. None of them edited the interface plan. This note records the conflicts that the local test screen had to settle.

The banner is `#next-action`. It stays in normal flow and also sticks to the top, with no scrim and no dialog over the map. Its colors are the next-action review: `#F4F0E6` on `#1A1814`. Page labels stay `#1A1A1A` on `#FFFFFF`.

Nation colors are the composition review, because those pairs clear 4.5:1 by a wider margin: Russia `#5E2B8A`, China `#F5C400` with `#1A1A1A` text and border, India `#1A1A1A`, Brazil `#146C2E`, United States `#B42318`, Europe `#1D4E89`. The map uses one letter, R C I B U E, plus the pattern named in that review. Investor panels use RU CN IN BR US EU.

A choice the acting player cannot pay from personal cash is dark only on that player's screen. Other seats receive it as an ordinary public cost. The next-action review had spectators see a dashed unpaid rondel space. That was not built. A dashed space would show the table a bound on secret cash, which the rondel review and the panel review both forbid.

Banner buttons use the next-action words: Confirm move, Cancel, Place here, Stop on Investor, Let it pass, Commit taxation, Commit production. The map wheel is not a click target. The action rondel is the choice list beside the map.

The schematic does not copy the publisher illustration. It draws only the borders named in the booklet or the Quick Intro. Panama's west mouth is not drawn. Switzerland is labeled and is not a move target.
